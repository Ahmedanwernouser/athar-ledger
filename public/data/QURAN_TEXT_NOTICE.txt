In this project:
 - quran_display.json holds the text verbatim, as received, and is the ONLY Quran text shown to users, including the
   "in the source" line of a word-by-word comparison and every suggestion. The basmala that the file attaches to the
   first verse of a sura is shown on its own line, with no character changed.
 - passages_*.json and idx*.bin hold a normalised SEARCH INDEX derived from it (diacritics removed, letter forms
   unified, the basmala detached from the first verse of each sura). It is used for matching only and is never
   presented as the text of the Quran.
